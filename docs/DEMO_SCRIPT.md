# MallWay five-minute presentation and live demo

MallWay is intended for malls across Malaysia. KL East Mall is the current demonstration dataset. Allow 55 seconds for the introduction slide and 4 minutes 5 seconds for the live system. Screen actions are not spoken.

## 0:00-0:55 Introduction slide

**Screen actions:** Keep the introduction slide on screen.

Hello everyone. Today I'm presenting MallWay, a web-based indoor navigation and parking assistance system intended for malls across Malaysia. A visitor may know which shop they want, but still need to choose a parking lobby, find the correct lift, and reach the right floor. MallWay aims to bring these decisions into one platform. It supports planning before arrival, starting after parking, or confirming a landmark inside the mall, followed by a route back to saved parking. Our current working demonstration uses KL East Mall. I'll now show the complete visitor journey using this demo.

## 0:55-1:15 Open the system and find a shop

**Screen actions:** Switch to the browser. Point to the three starting modes on Navigate. Open Shop Directory, search Absolute Thai, then click Directions on its card.

The home page supports three starting situations: Plan a visit, I've parked, and I'm inside. For this example, I want to visit Absolute Thai. I can find it through Shop Directory and select Directions to begin planning.

## 1:15-2:10 Compare parking approaches and create a route

**Screen actions:** Choose Plan a visit. Open Compare 2 other approaches briefly, then keep West lobby approach selected. Enable Step-free route. Click Find my route, then Save parking. Wait for the parking-saved message.

Because I am planning before arrival, MallWay compares the available parking-lobby approaches in its route network. Each option shows an estimated walking distance and journey time to the selected shop. These suggestions help me choose a convenient lobby, but they do not show live parking-bay availability. I'll use the West lobby approach and enable Step-free route, which uses lifts for floor changes. After selecting Find my route, the system displays the journey on the published floor plan. I'll also save this parking checkpoint so that I can return to the same lobby later.

## 2:10-3:10 Explain the map and lift directions

**Screen actions:** On B, point to the blue START pin. Click Confirm my start. Point to West lift lobby and B to G1. Click Exit G1 to preview the destination floor, then Focus route. Point to the red pin, amber lift and blue arrows. Click I've reached G1 to advance to the walking step.

The blue pin identifies the selected starting checkpoint. After I confirm it, the next instruction names the West lift lobby and tells me to board on Basement and select G1. The amber marker makes the lift easier to identify on the map. Exit G1 lets me preview the floor where I should leave the lift. Here, the red pin marks Absolute Thai's destination approach, while the blue line and arrows show the walking direction. Focus route brings this part of the map closer. I confirm reaching G1 before continuing. These buttons advance the instructions manually; the phone is not tracking my movement indoors.

## 3:10-3:55 Update the current starting landmark

**Screen actions:** Click Update start. Select G1 as CURRENT FLOOR. Select Kenangan Coffee, G1-29, as NEAREST SHOP OR LANDMARK. Click Confirm my start and show the recalculated route to Absolute Thai.

If I open the system from another location inside the mall, I can update my starting point. I select my current floor and a nearby shop or landmark, such as Kenangan Coffee on G1. After confirmation, MallWay keeps Absolute Thai as my destination and calculates a route from the new starting point. This is useful when I begin somewhere different from the original parking lobby. The visitor confirms the landmark they can actually see.

## 3:55-4:40 Return to saved parking

**Screen actions:** Open My Parking. Show the saved West lobby approach. Add the note Near the west lift and click Save parking. In WHERE ARE YOU NOW?, select G1, Absolute Thai. Click Route to saved parking. Point to the G1 to B lift instruction in the step list.

After shopping, I open My Parking. The saved lobby remains available, and I can add a note to help remember where I parked. To return, I choose my current landmark and select Route to saved parking. MallWay generates the reverse journey, including the lift from G1 back to Basement. The destination is the saved lobby checkpoint. My note helps with the final walk to the car, because the prototype does not position individual parking bays.

## 4:40-5:00 Closing

**Screen actions:** Keep the return route visible. Stop clicking and address the audience.

MallWay's goal is to make mall journeys clearer across Malaysia. KL East Mall demonstrates the current workflow. Each additional mall needs its own validated maps and route data. Our next step is field validation before wider coverage. Thank you.

Rehearse with a timer. If running late, skip comparing other parking approaches and typing a parking note. Physical access and lift links still require field validation.
